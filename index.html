The Problem

Charter operators fly blind.

Private aviation is an asset-heavy, margin-thin business. Operators manage complex multi-aircraft fleets with scheduling tools built for the past decade — and no predictive layer. The result is revenue left on the runway.

↗ Revenue Loss

Empty Legs Sold at a Loss

Repositioning flights go unsold or are priced by gut feel — operators lack dynamic demand signals to capture marginal revenue from otherwise dead miles.

~35% of all private jet flights carry zero passengers
⚙ Operations Risk

Reactive Maintenance Cycles

Scheduled maintenance is static. AOG (Aircraft on Ground) incidents hit without warning, triggering charter cancellations, passenger compensation, and crew disruption cascades.

Average AOG downtime: 3–5 days per incident
✓ Compliance Burden

Manual Safety Compliance

Passenger safety records, crew certification tracking, and regulatory audits are managed across spreadsheets and email chains — prone to gaps that risk AOC violations.

FAR Part 135 violations carry $25,000+ penalties
⛽ Cost Volatility

Fuel Costs Caught Operators Off-Guard

Jet-A pricing swings with geopolitics and demand cycles. Operators price long-horizon charters without a fuel cost model, eroding margins on trips booked months out.

Fuel = 30–40% of total charter operating cost

The Founder

Built by someone who understands operational risk.

AP
Aditya Pal
Founder & CEO, Pivotara
"Charter operators don't have a data problem — they have a decision problem. The data exists. No one has turned it into the right signal at the right time."

Aditya Pal founded Pivotara after identifying a systemic gap in how private aviation operators manage revenue and risk. With a background spanning aviation operations, data engineering, and B2B SaaS, he spent two years embedded with charter operators across the US and EMEA — mapping the exact failure modes that cause empty legs to go unsold, maintenance to become reactive, and compliance to slip through the cracks.

The insight driving Pivotara is straightforward: the private aviation industry has abundant operational data locked in siloed systems — scheduling software, MRO logs, fuel receipts, flight manifests. Pivotara's purpose is to unlock that data and translate it into predictive decisions that operators can act on, before problems become costs.
